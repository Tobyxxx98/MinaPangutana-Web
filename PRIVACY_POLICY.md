# Privacy Policy for MinaPangutana

Effective date: September 20, 2026

MinaPangutana is an independent educational reviewer developed by Cristopher Gonzaga. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or an official application of the Professional Regulation Commission, the Board of Mining Engineering, or any government agency.

## Data stored on the device

MinaPangutana stores question banks, imported study content, answers, and related app data locally on the user's device. The current version does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit this study data to the developer.

## Imported content

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to import and use third-party questions or study materials. Third-party content remains the property of its respective owner.

## Data deletion

Users may remove stored questions using the Clear All function. Uninstalling the app or clearing its website data may also remove locally stored information.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when MinaPangutana adds services that collect or transmit information. The published policy should always describe the behavior of the released version.
